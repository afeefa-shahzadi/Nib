# Mycelium User Flows

## Purpose
This document describes the major end-to-end user flows for creators, readers, members, and editors on the Mycelium platform.

## Creator Flow: Draft to Multimodal Publish
1. Creator opens the studio dashboard.
2. Creator starts a new content object.
3. System offers archive-based suggestions and related objects.
4. Creator writes using structured blocks.
5. Creator previews skim, standard, expert, teach, and debate modes.
6. Creator adds graph relationships and optional monetization settings.
7. Creator publishes the primary version.
8. Creator optionally creates derivative branches such as audio, teaching pack, or member debate branch.

## Reader Flow: Discovery to Deep Engagement
1. Reader discovers a public piece from search, link, or direct visit.
2. Reader enters the default reading mode.
3. Reader switches to another mode based on intent or curiosity.
4. Reader explores linked branches or related graph objects.
5. Reader chooses whether to subscribe, join discussion, or purchase a bundle.

## Member Flow: Paid Access and Discussion
1. Member signs in.
2. Member accesses premium branches or debate mode.
3. Member reads member-only content.
4. Member joins claim-level or section-level discussions.
5. Member saves branches or follows a topic cluster.

## Editor Flow: Review and Publish
1. Editor opens draft workspace.
2. Editor reviews structure, evidence, and mode outputs.
3. Editor leaves comments or annotations.
4. Editor approves, requests changes, or schedules publishing.

## Bundle Flow: Archive to Product
1. Creator opens bundle builder.
2. System recommends related content objects and branches.
3. Creator selects assets for the bundle.
4. Creator configures title, description, pricing, and access model.
5. Creator publishes bundle as a premium knowledge product.
