# Mycelium Monetization Model

## Purpose
This document defines how Mycelium can support creator revenue in a way that is broader than standard subscription-only blogging tools.[web:1][web:3]

## Monetization Layers
### 1. Open Layer
Public publishing supports discovery, SEO presence, brand building, and audience growth.

### 2. Patron Layer
Creators can gate premium branches, debate rooms, early drafts, and member-only variants.

### 3. Knowledge Layer
Creators can sell bundles, archive packs, mini-courses, curated learning journeys, and research collections.

### 4. Presence Layer
The platform also acts as a creator website and professional knowledge hub, indirectly increasing consulting, speaking, hiring, and collaboration opportunities.

## Revenue Objects
- Membership tiers.
- One-time bundle purchases.
- Premium branches.
- Cohort or class access.
- Sponsored collections in later phases.

## Monetization Rules
- Premium access should apply at mode, branch, room, or bundle level.
- Monetization should never break canonical content identity.
- Public value should remain strong enough to sustain discovery.
- Paid surfaces should feel additive, not punitive.

## Success Metrics
- Paid conversion rate by branch type.
- Bundle attachment rate.
- Revenue mix across memberships and products.
- Retention rate of paying members.
- Average revenue per knowledge cluster.
